Dengyi Liu

Ph.D. student in Computer Science, Yeshiva University
Research Scientist Intern, TCL CSOT America

I work on efficient generative AI for computer vision: making diffusion and flow models small and fast enough for places where compute and bandwidth are tight. Recent work rebuilds images from caption, edge and color streams sent over low-power wireless links, and fills in missing clinical trial data with federated flow matching. I'm advised by Prof. Honggang Wang and Prof. Hua Fang.

Research

Diffusion transformers for super-resolution

Making DiT-based image super-resolution fast and compact enough for high-resolution, real-time use, by distilling large diffusion models into small few-step DiTs while keeping the generated detail true to the input.

Generative image delivery for low-power IoT

Sending an image as separate caption, edge and color streams over low-power wide-area links and rebuilding it with a generative decoder, with each stream protected according to how much the decoder relies on it.

Flow matching for missing health data

Mask-aware and federated flow matching that imputes missing longitudinal trial measurements across sites while patient records stay where they are.

Medical imaging and health

Diffusion-based segmentation of polyp and prostate images, and video language models that score caregiver–child play for occupational therapists.
